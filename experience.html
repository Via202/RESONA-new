How it works

REMIX

  • Select a soundtrack → Play.
  • Or upload your own audio → Play.
  • Choose Processed to adjust the audio via Color, Distortion, Space and Energy controls.
  • Choose Original to hear the recording without those effects.

CREATE

  • Choose an environment → Generate → Play.
  • Generate again to create a different song.

CONTROLS

  • Live: Changes the sound immediately in CREATE.
  • Next Generate: Adjust setting → Generate → Stop → Play.

PLAYBACK

  • Play / Pause: Start or pause the music.
  • Resume: Continue where you paused.
  • Stop: Reset to the beginning.
  • Restart: Play the same song from the start.
